# Discovery kit — putting Phase 2 in the hands of real QA / RA

This directory holds the working material for the discovery
phase: structured 30-minute interviews with people whose daily
job already involves the frameworks Phase 2 maps onto. The goal
is **not** to sell openMiura; the goal is to learn whether the
governance pattern in [`../whitepaper.md`](../whitepaper.md)
matches a real pain — and *which* pain, *for whom*, *how often*,
*with what current workaround*.

## Files

- [`target_personas.md`](target_personas.md) — six personas
  worth talking to, with the jobs they actually do and the
  stakes they actually carry.
- [`discovery_questions.md`](discovery_questions.md) — the
  interview script, Mom Test style: past behaviour and concrete
  pain, never hypothetical or solution-led.
- [`outreach_templates.md`](outreach_templates.md) —
  short messages for LinkedIn / email asking for 30 minutes,
  in Spanish and English.
- [`interview_log_template.md`](interview_log_template.md) —
  one-page template Curro fills in after each conversation, so
  the conversations remain comparable across personas.

## Method

The interviews are deliberately **listening exercises**, not
demos:

1. The interviewer asks Mom-Test-style questions about the past.
2. The interviewer takes notes against the
   [`interview_log_template.md`](interview_log_template.md).
3. The interviewer **does not** show openMiura unless the
   interviewee explicitly asks for an example, and even then
   only at the end and only briefly.
4. After 5–10 conversations, patterns emerge: who has the pain,
   how often, what they do today, what would change if they
   had a credible alternative.

Output of the discovery phase feeds directly into Phase 4
(\`docs/STRATEGY.md\` — three routes plus a recommendation
based on the discovery data).

## Why this matters

The Phase 2 whitepaper and mappings are *internally consistent*
but they have not been validated against real users. A
governance plane is only useful if there is a real audience
with a real pain that openMiura's controls match. The discovery
kit is the cheapest way to find out before committing to either
of the strategic routes (academic / open-source niche / spin-off).
