# Teaching materials around openMiura

Two documents in this directory, both targeted at university
teaching at the master / final-year-undergraduate level:

- [`lecture_governance_llm_agents.md`](lecture_governance_llm_agents.md)
  — 90-minute lecture script: *Governance of LLM agents in
  regulated scientific environments*. Designed to be delivered
  once, reusable as a module inside a broader course on AI for
  laboratory science, biomedical engineering or pharmaceutical
  technology.
- [`tfg_tfm_proposals.md`](tfg_tfm_proposals.md) — five concrete
  TFG (Trabajo Fin de Grado) and TFM (Trabajo Fin de Máster)
  proposals derived from the openMiura platform. Each proposal
  has clearly stated learning objectives, deliverables and
  scope, so a student can self-assess fit before committing.

## Audience and prerequisites

Suitable for:

- Final-year undergraduates in Chemistry, Pharmacy, Biomedical
  Engineering, Computer Science with a software-engineering
  track.
- Master students in Pharmaceutical Technology, Quality
  Assurance, Regulatory Affairs, Computational Chemistry, Data
  Science applied to the laboratory.

Prerequisites for the lecture: basic knowledge of databases,
basic familiarity with what an LLM is and what an "agent" is in
the LLM sense. No regulatory background assumed; the lecture
introduces 21 CFR Part 11 / Annex 11 / GAMP 5 at the level
needed.

Prerequisites for each TFG/TFM are stated in
[`tfg_tfm_proposals.md`](tfg_tfm_proposals.md).

## How to reuse

These materials are released under the same Apache-2.0 licence
as the openMiura repository. Adapt freely to the institution's
calendar and assessment style; cite the project URL where useful.
