### Decisions & rationale

- **Why also edit the mapping table, not just the paper.** The paper
  cites `docs/regulated/` as its authoritative table. Making the paper
  assert "§11.200 improved" while the table still said `Experimental`
  would replace one staleness with a worse inconsistency. The features
  genuinely shipped and are tested, and DoD §7 requires honest tables,
  so the honest move is to update both together.

  surgical (add what shipped, correct what's stale), not a rewrite.
- **Scope held.** Docs-only change. No code, no dependencies, no public
  API. `references.bib` left untouched (audited clean, no fabrication).
